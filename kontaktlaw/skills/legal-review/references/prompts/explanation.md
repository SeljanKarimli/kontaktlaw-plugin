# Explanation

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Explain who must do what, when, under which conditions and with what consequence. Read relevant exceptions and definitions. Distinguish the clause meaning from a recommendation to change it. Only discuss law when needed and supported.

## Example

A penalty applies after written notice plus a cure period: explain both prerequisites, not merely the daily rate.
