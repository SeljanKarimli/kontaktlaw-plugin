# Grammar

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Correct spelling, agreement, punctuation and objectively defective syntax. A drafting error here means a linguistic defect whose correction preserves legal effect. Do not alter negation, party roles, defined terms, names, numbers, dates, conditions, rights or duties. Separate style preferences and substantive ambiguities from grammar; do not silently repair them. Verify each replacement in the complete sentence.

## Example

“Supplier are not required to insure the goods” becomes “Supplier is not required to insure the goods”; retain “not”.
