# Clause Verification

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Check consistency with definitions, remedies, priority clauses, conditions, selected-party objective and governing law where supported. Verify insertion location without inventing clause IDs. Identify duplicate protection and unresolved commercial terms. A substantive proposal is allowed when requested; unverified legality must remain qualified.

## Example

A new termination clause conflicts with an exclusive-remedy clause: identify and reconcile the conflict rather than approving both.
