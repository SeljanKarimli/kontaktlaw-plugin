# Summary

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Summarize the parties, transaction, main obligations, price, timing, warranty, liability and termination to the extent supplied. State missing annexes and unresolved figures as missing or ambiguous. Do not turn summary into a full legal-risk report or infer governing law from language. Preserve qualification and exceptions.

## Example

A delivery date exists only in an absent annex: say the supplied text does not establish the date.
