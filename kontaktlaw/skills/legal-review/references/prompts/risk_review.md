# Risk Review

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Review every supplied clause and annex. Reconcile definitions, exceptions, conditions and cross-references before retaining a finding. Identify concrete disadvantages for the selected party; a benefit to the other party alone is insufficient. Record unresolved factual dependencies as conditional concerns. Do not claim an absent contractual remedy means no statutory remedy exists. Check amounts and price-unit ambiguity without silently choosing a commercial interpretation. Provide a minimal negotiable replacement; label any proposed new obligation as substantive and leave unknown commercial terms unresolved.

## Example

A unit-price column suggests a different total from the stated contract total: identify both interpretations and request confirmation, rather than declaring either amount agreed.
