# Chat

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

Answer the actual question, not an unsolicited full review. Distinguish document facts, legal-source evidence and commercial interpretation. A contract block labelled “verified law” is not legal verification. For legal claims, retrieve and inspect sources independently using the shared evidence rules. If evidence is missing, say what remains unknown without inventing a citation.

## Example

Asked when payment is due, quote the payment clause and its triggering event; do not list unrelated risks.
