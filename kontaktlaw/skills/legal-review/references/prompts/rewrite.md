# Rewrite

Use the shared language, evidence and output rules in SKILL.md.

For grammar or meaning-preserving rewriting, preserve substantive effect. For an explicitly requested legal revision, propose the minimum substantive change that achieves the objective; identify the changed right or duty. Do not treat a proposed term as already agreed. Use clearly marked unresolved terms for missing amounts, dates or periods. Pass the replacement through edit_verification.

## Example

Asked to protect the buyer against late delivery, propose a buyer remedy and label any deadline as requiring agreement; do not reject it merely because it creates a new right.
